Objections, Answered.

Everything you need to know about the premium resting suite for your currency.

How does the weight-activated LED underglow work?

The bed contains a hidden precision snap-fit leaf switch integrated underneath the recessed platform. When your wallet is rested, the weight triggers the contact, activating a soft, warm ambient LED underglow that functions as a bedside nightlight for your money.

Will my specific wallet fit the platform?

Absolutely. The Wallet Bed™ is precision-contoured and optimized to seamlessly accommodate all standard bi-fold and tri-fold wallets, keeping your cards securely framed and accessible while maintaining a sleek, luxury bedroom aesthetic on your nightstand.

Does it require batteries or plugins?

The base model features a bottom-drop hollow cavity housing a 3x AA battery pack, eliminating ugly wires on your desk or nightstand. Advanced future variants are scheduled to support RGB plugins and Qi wireless charging integration.
