Everything you need to know about the premium resting suite for your essentials.

How does the automated nightlight work?

The WalletBed contains a built-in automated nightlight sensor. When you approach or when the room goes dark, it automatically illuminates your essentials, providing a soft bedside light during your evening routine without disrupting the room.

Will my specific wallet fit the platform?

Absolutely. The Wallet Bed™ is precision-contoured and optimized to seamlessly accommodate all standard bi-fold and tri-fold wallets, keeping your cards securely framed and accessible while maintaining a sleek, luxury bedroom aesthetic on your nightstand.

Does it require batteries or plugins?

The base model features a bottom-drop hollow cavity housing a coin-cell battery or rechargeable battery module, eliminating ugly wires on your nightstand. It is highly optimized for clean bedside and workspace placement.

When will my order ship?

We are currently completing design and prototyping phases. Orders will ship according to standard flat-rate checkout shipping methods ($5.99 Standard Shipping for single units, or $11.99 Premium Tracked Shipping for our 3-Pack Bundle).
